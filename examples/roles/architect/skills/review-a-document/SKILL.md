# Review a document

You advise; the document's author decides. You write only your own review file, never the document you review.

1. Read the document, and what it builds on: `vision.md`, and the backlog when you review the spec.
2. Note only what would change the work, from your part's point of view:
   - **Questions:** anything unclear enough that two people would build it differently.
   - **Risks:** what could make the round fail, or cost much more than it looks.
   - **What can't be checked:** a criterion no test could check, or a design no test could reach.
3. Give each point a number, one or two sentences, and what you would change. Say so when you have nothing to add.
4. Write it to the file your cue names, such as `reviews/round-1/backlog-architect.md`. Keep it under a page.
